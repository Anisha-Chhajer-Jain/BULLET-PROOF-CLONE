What is Cold Brew?

Cold brew is a refreshing take on traditional hot-brewed coffee. The brewing method involves steeping coarse-ground coffee with cold water for 12-24 hours. This slow extraction process creates a smooth, less acidic and full-bodied brew. Taste is just the tip of the iceberg—you can customize cold brew to your preference and batch-prep it ahead of time to stay cool all week long.
